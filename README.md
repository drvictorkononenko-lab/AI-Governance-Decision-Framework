# AI Governance & Decision Intelligence Framework

This framework provides a structured, risk-based approach to managing the full lifecycle of Enterprise AI initiatives. It is designed to bridge the gap between technical MLOps and business value preservation.

## The 5-Gate Value Chain

1. **Gate 1: Triage & Signal** – Initial filtering of AI use cases based on readiness and economic potential.
2. **Gate 2: Architecture & Risk** – Assessment of integration complexity and "architectural burden."
3. **Gate 3: Validation Protocol** – Transitioning from MVP to Production through Shadow Mode and "Trust Underwriting."
4. **Gate 4: Economic ROI Model** – Final verification of economic viability and unit economics.
5. **Gate 5: Day-2 Operations** – Continuous governance, drift management, and safety "Kill Switches."
